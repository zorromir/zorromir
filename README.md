# Kelvin Schouten

**MSc Quantitative Finance · BSc Mathematics**

I am a Quantitative Finance master's student at Tilburg University, with a background in mathematics from Utrecht University.

My interests lie in applying mathematical modelling, statistics, and computational methods to financial problems. I enjoy starting with a question, examining the assumptions behind a model, and exploring its behaviour through mathematical analysis and numerical experiments.

This GitHub profile contains selected academic, research, and independent projects.

## Selected projects

**[Fractional Harmonic Oscillator](https://github.com/zorromir/fractional-harmonic-oscillator)**  
Numerical investigation of fractional-order differential equations, based on my bachelor's thesis.

**[Pipeline Flow Modelling](https://github.com/zorromir/pipeline-flow-modelling)**  
Mathematical modelling and computational analysis of two-phase flow in industrial pipelines.

**[Yield Curve Modelling](https://github.com/zorromir/yield-curve-modelling)**  
Exploring yield curves, discount factors, bond pricing and stochastic interest-rate models, including Vasicek and Monte Carlo methods.

**[What Do Panel Estimators Recover?](https://github.com/zorromir/What-Do-Panel-Estimators-Recover-)**  
A short simulation study comparing panel-data estimators when unobserved individual effects are correlated with regressors. [Python simulation](https://github.com/zorromir/What-Do-Panel-Estimators-Recover-/blob/main/code/panel_monte_carlo.py).

**[Testing Financial Factor Models](https://zorromir.github.io/assets/reports/Empirical_finance.pdf)**  
An MSc group study comparing CAPM and Fama–French factor models using industry portfolio returns, with attention to model fit, pricing errors and econometric inference.
